# Farming and gathering on the map

At dawn in Ica, Today points Amru down the lane to his maize field. At the headland he can **Loosen a planting row** or **Clear the weedy edge**. A neighbour starts on the next row while Amru bends, pulls a clump and steps along the edge. He can leave halfway. Walking home that evening, he can see exactly where he stopped.

## Two playable starts

- **Maize cultivator in Ica, 246 CE**, seed `ica-field-review`. The household had a maize field about 46 tiles from home. The first hour ended beside an earthen pot. After a plot interruption, the player reached the field and got a card offering deep ploughing, shallow ploughing or a neighbour's help. The recipe had already decided the job. Ordinary preparation advances the clock. Ordinary sowing and weeding record a season. Draft-team passes and manual reaping already change individual cells. [Recipes](../../src/content/economy/recipes.ts) · [Farm cards](../../src/content/cards/farm.ts) · [Field work](../../src/core/engine.ts)
- **Gatherer in Normandy, 9000 BCE**, seed `woodland-food-review`. Finding a patch and gathering both pointed to one grass tile. Fallen wood was the only resource within four tiles. A plot announced a full bag while inventory still showed the two fruit the player started with. The base Gatherer does “Gathering supplies”, while the seasonal recipe matches “Gathering plants”. Both use a wild destination chosen independently of what grows there. [Livelihoods](../../src/content/characters/livelihoods.ts) · [Work places](../../src/core/work-places.ts) · [Gathering plot](../../src/content/plots/glut.ts)
- **The neighbours.** Three Ica farmers each had 33 scheduled minutes of tending. The ordinary itinerary aims for 18% of the day outdoors and caps each tending stop at 11 minutes. Farmers visit crop objects owned by the worker, so hired hands can spend their field stop at the entrance. Scheduled stations set a pose and carried item. A separate household routine can actually pick and deposit food, but a fed resident following their itinerary usually bypasses it. ## Build order

**1. Start at a real field.** Replace the cultivator's seed opener with a walk from home to the household's or employer's parcel. Pin its entrance and a short run of reachable cells in the existing workday. If a farmer's holding failed placement, first try a neighbouring holding that employs them. Fix agricultural starts that have neither. Retire the fallback that names arbitrary open ground “your plot”.

At home, the Today work row offers **Work your field · Go**. On the assigned parcel or its workable edge, the same row offers **Choose fieldwork**, including when the player walks there independently. Pressing F or clicking Choose opens the existing numbered work-event card at the foot of the map. Escape returns to the field with the choice still available. Selecting a job closes the card and puts the job and its progress in the Today row. The errands and obligations stay visible throughout. Offer two or three jobs from the local farm system, crop stage, equipment and actual cells. Each choice should name its immediate tradeoff. “Clear the weedy edge” protects the standing crop. “Open a planting row” makes room for more maize and uses more of the morning. Use visible grass clumps on the margin for weeding and open cells for planting. If only one job is possible, say why and let the player take it or leave. Store the choice and targets with the day so Today, F, touch and the agent all follow it.

**2. Make one row satisfying.** Build the Ica hand-work first. One press works a reachable clump or cell. Hold F or tap Work to walk and finish a short pass of roughly four to six strokes. Time and fatigue accrue per stroke. The tool touches the target as the tuft comes away or the furrow opens. Movement interrupts the pass. The marked row, remaining weeds and ledger carry the result forward. A completed edge earns a different offer the next morning. [Ground edits](../../src/core/tile-edits.ts) · [Character poses](../../src/render/characters/poses.ts)

Reuse the digging stick already drawn for Andean workers and the existing digging, tending and cutting poses. A planting pass spends household seed once and leaves a sown strip. Harvest leaves stubble and a load to take home. Where a dry cereal holding has oxen, use its existing plough pass. Where a flooded rice bed has a buffalo, use its puddling and transplanting actions. Then add watering and ditch clearing at a real water source, with a few cells of darkening soil, and manuring when material is available. [Farm profiles](../../src/content/agriculture/systems.ts) · [Draft teams](../../src/world/v3/conveyances.ts) · [Local implements and loads](../../src/content/economy/carrying.ts)

Use the parcel cells as the crop record. On the Ica seed, three sampled crop objects each held three grain on maize cells marked “sown”. Move seasonal readiness, NPC row targets and harvesting onto the same cells, then remove the duplicate field objects. Keep wild plants on their existing resource system. Scope sowing and weed care to the worked parcel. Reconcile the visible harvest with hourly household production so each crop enters stock once, in the appropriate good. [Parcel generation](../../src/world/v3/plan.ts) · [Household economy](../../src/core/economy.ts)

**3. Let neighbours do the same work.** Give farmers a sustained daylight work period in the existing itinerary. Stagger departures and give workers on the same holding adjacent strips. Only advance a scheduled pass when the worker reaches its target. Apply the same edit a player would make. If a cell has already been worked, move to the next one. Preserve the existing routine budget and account for elapsed work once when time skips. Judge the result from the lane during an ordinary morning.

**4. Send gatherers after food.** Match the base Gatherer to the seasonal recipe by livelihood ID. Reuse the forage errand's search for reachable, edible, in-season resources. Offer the near patch for a quick return or a longer circuit with more food. If a patch is bare, offer another reachable food source or let the player return empty. Start with existing fruit trees and berry patches, then extend seasonal greens, nuts and roots where the ecology supports them. Picking should strip the fruit, leave the plant and fill the basket. The carrier can turn home early and unload just the outing's food. Use the same harvest and deposit for NPCs. Tie the “bag is full” story and the laden basket art to what the carrier actually picked. [Forage errand](../../src/runtime/autopilot.ts) · [Wild resources](../../src/world/v3/population.ts) · [Harvest and deposit](../../src/core/livelihood.ts)

Prove the local round first. Then extend it through a reachable land exit when the return fits the day. Map travel currently carries the player and their possessions. Give a travelling gatherer a home map, a destination patch and a return time. Track the outing while the far map is unloaded. If the player follows, they should find the same person and patch, with the same load. Debit the patch and deliver the basket once. Verify one border before extending a route across two. [Map travel](../../src/runtime/map-travel.ts)

## Acceptance

- Start in Ica through the normal game. Walk to the field, play each choice on separate runs, interrupt halfway and return the following morning. Inspect the result at normal zoom with the HUD closed. Repeat on a dry ox-ploughed holding and a flooded rice holding.
- Watch an ordinary farmer take a row. Work a cell ahead of them and wait through their shift. Check the ground, the carried load, stock and ledger, including across a time skip. Check that sown maize cannot also be picked as ripe grain.
- Follow a gatherer out empty and back laden. Pick a patch ahead of them, turn home early, and return before regrowth. Repeat across a border when that phase lands.
- Extend the existing livelihood, farmland and economy tests with connected starts and ordinary commands. Check keyboard and touch in the browser, inspect before-and-after frames at play scale, and run `npm run check` on the implementation.
