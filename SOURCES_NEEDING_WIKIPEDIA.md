## Implementation Priority

### High Priority (Visual/Archaeological)
1. Cave paintings (Lascaux, Serra da Capivara, Monte Alegre, Blombos)
2. Archaeological sites (Göbekli Tepe, Cahokia, Tiwanaku Gate)
3. Ancient artifacts (Moche ceramics, Yangshao pottery, Lapita pottery)
4. Mysterious scripts (Rongorongo, Behistun Inscription)

### Medium Priority (Famous Texts with Visual Elements)
1. Codices (Mendoza, Florentine, Popol Vuh)
2. Ancient inscriptions (Code of Hammurabi, Gudea Cylinders)
3. Sacred texts with archaeological context (Book of the Dead)

### Lower Priority (Literary Texts)
- These are primarily textual and may not benefit as much from Wikipedia's visual content
- Epic of Gilgamesh, Iliad, Odyssey, etc. (better suited for Wikisource)

## Notes
- Focus on sources where visual content (images, maps, diagrams) would enhance understanding
- Archaeological sites and artifacts are the best candidates
- Ancient texts are good candidates if they have famous physical representations (stone tablets, papyri, etc.)
- Pure literary works should continue using Wikisource integration
